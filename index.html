Vegas 6-6-6 Rules

  • 18 holes split into 3 segments of 6. You pick First 6 teams now; Middle 6 after hole 6.
  • Each team's net scores combine into a 2-digit Vegas number (lower first).
  • Net birdie or better → opponent's digits are reversed.
  • Net score: player gets −1 stroke on holes where their handicap ≥ hole's handicap index.
  • Each segment scores independently. Grand total = sum of all 3 segments.
